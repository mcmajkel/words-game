# 🚀 How to Publish Your Game Online (2 Minutes)

## Step 1: Open Netlify Drop
Go to: **https://app.netlify.com/drop**

## Step 2: Drag & Drop Your File
Drag the file `game.html` (or `index.html`) from your computer into the browser window.

You'll see a big upload area - just drop the file there!

## Step 3: Wait for Upload
The file will upload automatically. You'll see:
- A progress bar
- Then a success message
- A live URL appears!

## Step 4: Get Your Live URL
You'll get a URL like:
```
https://random-name-12345.netlify.app
```

**Copy this URL and share it with anyone!**

---

## Optional: Create Free Account (To Keep Site Forever)

Without an account, the site stays live for 24 hours. To keep it permanent:

1. Click "Get your own site" button
2. Sign up with email (takes 30 seconds)
3. Verify email
4. Your site is now permanent!

---

Your game is now online and accessible from:

Share the URL and anyone can play your game!
